04 — About

I started out as a CS student who loved machine learning, Arduino and graphic design in equal measure. These days that curiosity lives in hardware that has to work the first time it leaves the ground.

I like owning a problem end-to-end — from a pin map on a flight controller to the dashboard someone uses to ship the finished product. Creativity and authenticity are what I try to bring to every build.

Off the clock: hip-hop culture and street dance (first place, Anugoonj group street-dance competition), football, and re-watching HIMYM more times than I'll admit.
